Reviews and scheduling

Study modes, grades and how FSRS schedules reviews.

Three ways to study

Study on a deck starts a review of the cards that are due. The menu next to it holds two practice modes that never touch your schedule.

ReviewShows the cards that are due. Every grade updates that card's schedule.
CramRuns through a deck's due cards, or all of them, without changing anything. Handy right before an exam.
TestYou type each answer, reveal it and grade yourself Missed, Close or Got it. The score screen compares with your last try and offers a retake of the ones you missed.

Keys in a review

On a Mac, use Cmd for Ctrl. You can change all of these under Settings, then Keyboard.

SpaceReveal the answer, press again for Good
1 to 4Again, Hard, Good, Easy
EEdit the current card without leaving
Ctrl ZUndo the last grade. Press again to step further back.
Leave the session

In a test, Enter reveals and then marks Got it, and 1 2 3 pick Missed, Close or Got it.

The four grades

Mnemo uses FSRS, the same open algorithm behind Anki. Each grade updates its guess of how well you know a card, and the card comes back just before you'd forget it.

AgainYou didn't remember.
HardYou got it, but slowly.
GoodYou remembered. This is the normal one.
EasyIt was trivial.

Each button shows when the card would come back. Grade honestly, because the schedule is only as good as your grades.

New cards and leeches

New cards go through a few short steps on the same day before they move to longer gaps. Again restarts the steps, Good moves on and Easy skips straight ahead. A card you forget later goes back through the steps to be relearned.

A card you forget eight times is marked as a leech and tagged leech. That's usually a sign the card needs rewriting. You can also have leeches suspended automatically.

Daily limits

Each deck brings in up to 20 new cards a day and up to 200 reviews. Cards you're still learning are never cut off. If reviews start piling up, lower the new cards first, since every new card adds future reviews.

The study day rolls over at 4 in the morning, not at midnight, so a late night session still counts for the day before. Missed a day? No problem, due cards simply wait for you.

Review settings

Open Review settings from a deck's menu. Settings live in presets that several decks can share, so editing one changes every deck that uses it.

New cards per day20 by default
Reviews per day200 by default
Desired retentionHow many reviews you aim to pass, from 80% to 97%. 90% by default. Higher means more reviews, lower means more forgetting.
Learning stepsThe short steps for new cards, like 1m 10m
Next day starts at4 in the morning by default
Lapse limitHow many times a card can be forgotten before it counts as a leech. 8 by default.

Fit FSRS to you

Once a preset's decks have at least 400 reviews, press Optimize to tune FSRS to your own memory. If the result predicts your recall better, Apply puts it to work straight away. Use defaults goes back to the built in settings.

Session options

  • Shuffle card order mixes up the queue.
  • Bury related cards holds back cards made from the same note until tomorrow, so three blanks from one sentence don't all show up at once. On by default.
  • Auto reveal shows the answer after 5 or 10 seconds.
