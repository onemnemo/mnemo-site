Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy covers the Mnemo app for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, and this website. Mnemo is made by the Mnemo contributors.

Summary

Mnemo does not collect, store or share personal data. You do not need an account. Your notes, mind maps, flashcards and review history are stored on your own device.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Mnemo is saved in a local database and folder on your device. This includes notes, mind maps, flashcards, images you add, review history and settings. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can export it, back it up or delete it at any time. Uninstalling the app does not delete your library folder; you can delete it yourself.

Data we collect

None. Mnemo has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Network connections

Mnemo works without an internet connection. The desktop app connects to the internet only to check for new versions. This check requests the latest release information from GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information. Mnemo does not send any of your content or any identifier with this request. GitHub's handling of this data is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell or transfer any user data, because we do not receive any. The only third party the app contacts is GitHub, for the update check described above.

Permissions

Mnemo only accesses files you choose to open, import or export, and its own library folder.

Children

Mnemo does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. See the Google Privacy Policy. Downloads are served from GitHub.

Future features

Planned features such as sync may involve sending data to a server. If that happens, this policy will be updated before the feature is released, and the feature will be optional.

Changes to this policy

Changes are published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to security@mnemo.one or posted as an issue on GitHub.
