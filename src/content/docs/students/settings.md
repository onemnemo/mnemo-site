Application settings are accessible from the **Settings** entry in the sidebar.

## Areas Covered by Settings

- **Appearance** — switch between light and dark themes.
- **Language** — change the display language.
- **Updates** — enable automatic update checks or trigger a manual check. See [Installation](/docs/students/installing#updates) for details on the update flow.
- **AI & Speech** — configure local model paths and speech features where applicable.
