Settings are grouped into categories in the sidebar of the **Settings** page. Changes apply immediately; there is no save button.

## Account

Your display name and profile picture. Both only affect the greeting and avatar inside the app. There is no account or sign-in.

## General

- **Launch at startup** starts Mnemo when you log in.
- **Toast notifications** controls the popup banners in the corner of the window. With toasts off, notifications collect behind the bell icon instead.
- **Language** switches the interface language: English, Spanish, German, Norwegian (Bokmål), or Japanese.
- **Gamification** shows an XP and streak badge in the top bar, fed by your study statistics.

## Editor

Writing options and typography for the note editor:

- Spellcheck on or off, plus which dictionary languages to use.
- Editor width.
- Typography controls: block spacing, line height, letter spacing, text size, code size, and math size.
- The **Auto save** toggle currently has no effect. Notes always save automatically.

## Appearance

Three built-in themes: **Dawn** (light, the default), **Noon** (warm light), and **Dusk** (dark). You can also pick an alternative app icon. There is no custom theme or accent color support.

## Mindmap

Canvas preferences: grid style and size, minimap visibility, and how modifier keys behave while dragging.

## AI Tools

Everything related to the experimental AI assistant: enabling it, downloading local models, GPU acceleration, and local knowledge indexing. See [AI assistant](/docs/students/ai-assistant).

## Updates

Automatic update checks and a manual **Check now** button. Your current version is shown here. See [Installation](/docs/students/installing#updates).

## Hotkeys

Opens the keybind manager. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/students/keyboard-shortcuts).
