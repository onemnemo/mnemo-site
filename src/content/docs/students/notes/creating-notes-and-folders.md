Use folders and notes to organize your study materials by subject, course, or topic.

## Creating a New Note

### From the Sidebar Menu

1. Open the Notes sidebar.
2. Locate the **My Notes** section.
3. Click the `+` icon.

### From the Context Menu

1. Open the Notes sidebar.
2. Hover over any item.
3. Click the **⋯** menu that appears on the right.
4. Select **New Note**.

## Creating a New Folder

1. Open the Notes sidebar.
2. Locate the **My Notes** section.
3. Click the `folder +` icon.
4. Rename the folder by double-clicking it.

Folders can contain notes and other folders.

## Renaming Notes and Folders

1. Hover over the note or folder.
2. Click the **⋯** menu.
3. Select **Rename**.
4. Enter the new name.

## Rearranging Items

Drag and drop notes and folders to reorganize them. You can:

- Move notes between folders
- Reorder notes within a folder
- Nest folders inside other folders

## Collapsing Folders

Click the arrow next to a folder to collapse or expand its contents. This helps keep large workspaces organized.

## Duplicating Notes

1. Hover over the note.
2. Click the **⋯** menu.
3. Select **Duplicate**.

The duplicated note will appear in the same folder.

## Deleting Notes and Folders

1. Hover over the note or folder.
2. Click the **⋯** menu.
3. Select **Delete**.

> **Warning:** Deleting a folder also deletes everything inside it.

## Sidebar Menu Options

Hover over any note or folder and click **⋯** to access:

- New Note
- New Folder
- Rename
- Duplicate
- Export
- Delete
