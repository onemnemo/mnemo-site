The **Flashcards** module stores decks and schedules reviews using configurable schedulers (FSRS, SM-2, Leitner, and others — exact availability follows build settings).

## Typical Workflow

1. Open **Flashcards** from the sidebar.
2. Pick or create a **deck**.
3. **Study** due cards; rate recall quality when prompted so the scheduler adjusts intervals.

## Cloze and Advanced Editing

Some builds support **cloze deletions** and keyboard chords wrapped around selection — behavior is tied to the deck editor and keybind router.
