Notes open in tabs along the top of the editor.

## Opening a note in a new tab

Right-click a note in the sidebar and choose **Open in new tab** to open it without leaving the current note. With a tab focused, the left and right arrow keys switch tabs, and `Home` and `End` jump to the first and last.

<!-- image idea: the notes editor with four tabs open above it, one active -->

## The tab menu

Right-click a tab, or press `Shift+F10` with it focused, for **Open in side peek**, **Close tab**, **Close other tabs**, **Close tabs to the left**, and **Close tabs to the right**. Middle-click a tab to close it directly.

**Open in side peek** moves the note into the side peek pane; see [The side peek](../../getting-started/side-peek.md).

## Closing tabs

Closing the last tab leaves the editor empty, and that note is not reopened next time.

Tabs are not saved between sessions. When you reopen Mnemo, you get the note you were last on, not the other tabs.
