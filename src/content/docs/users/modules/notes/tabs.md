Notes open in tabs along the top of the editor pane.

## Opening a note in the background

Right-click a note's row in the sidebar and choose **Open in new tab** to stage it without leaving the note you are reading. The left and right arrow keys move between tabs, and `Home` and `End` jump to the ends.

## The tab menu

Right-click a tab, or press `Shift+F10` or the Menu key while it has focus, for **Open in side peek**, **Close tab**, **Close other tabs**, **Close tabs to the left**, and **Close tabs to the right**.

**Open in side peek** moves the note into the side peek pane; see [Side peek](../../getting-started/side-peek.md) for what that pane does.

**Close other tabs**, **Close tabs to the left**, and **Close tabs to the right** are disabled when there is nothing on that side to close.

The middle mouse button closes a tab directly, without opening the menu.

## Closing a tab

Closing the last tab is read as a decision to be on no note: Mnemo shows the empty state and does not reopen that note next time.

Tabs are a session working set, not a saved layout: reopen Mnemo and you get the note you were last on, not the tabs around it.
