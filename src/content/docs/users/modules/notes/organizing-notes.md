The sidebar beside the editor holds your notes and folders.

## Notes and folders

Create a note with **New note**, from a folder's menu to put it in that folder, or with `Ctrl+N` (`Cmd+N` on macOS). New folders start at the top level; drag one onto another to nest it. Rename anything by double-clicking its name or from its menu.

## Drag and drop

- **Drop a note onto a folder** to move it inside.
- **Drop a note near the top or bottom edge of another note** to place it above or below.
- **Drop a folder onto another folder** to nest it, or near its edge to reorder.

## Favourites

Star a note from its row menu or from the breadcrumb bar above the editor. Favourites appear in their own section at the top of the sidebar, most recently used first.

## Deleting

**Delete Note** and **Delete Folder** are on the row menu. Deleting a folder deletes everything inside it. There is no confirmation: the item moves to the trash and a toast offers **Undo**. See [Trash](../../customization/trash.md) for restoring it later.

## Finding notes

- **By title.** `Ctrl+P` opens the sidebar search.
- **Inside a note.** `Ctrl+F` opens find and replace, with case-sensitive and whole-word options.
- **Within a long note.** The floating Index lists the note's headings; click one to jump to it.

Mnemo reopens the note you last had open.
