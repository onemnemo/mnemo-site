Welcome. These guides cover everything about using Mnemo day to day.

If you are brand new, start with [Getting started](./getting-started/index.md): it walks you from download to your first review session. After that, each module (flashcards, notes, mindmaps) has its own section under Modules with a first-steps guide and deeper how-tos.

Everything in Mnemo works offline and your data stays on your machine, so feel free to experiment; there is nothing here you can break that a fresh start will not fix.

Here to build on Mnemo rather than study with it? Switch to the [developer docs](../developers/index.md).
