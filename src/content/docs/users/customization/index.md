Mnemo runs on sensible defaults, and most of it can be tuned: theme, language, keyboard shortcuts, editor feel, and notifications. The same Settings window also holds your [profile](./profile.md), the one-file [backup and restore](./storage-and-backup.md), and the [trash](./trash.md) that catches everything you delete.
