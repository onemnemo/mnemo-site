Mnemo shows notifications as temporary toasts and keeps them available from the bell menu.

## What notifications are for

Notifications appear for things like:

* completed imports and exports
* PDF exports
* save errors
* available updates

<!-- image idea: the notification flyout open with the unread tab selected -->

## Toasts and the bell

Toasts appear briefly while you work.

The bell keeps recent notifications so you can come back to them after the toast disappears.

## Turn off toasts

Go to **Settings → Notifications** and turn off **Toasts**.

This hides the pop-up notifications, but messages will still appear in the bell menu.

Some notifications include actions or links. For example, update notifications can take you to **Settings → Updates**.
