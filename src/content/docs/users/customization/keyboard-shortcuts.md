Most shortcuts in Mnemo are rebindable. The manager lives in Settings, under Keyboard, and lists every action the app registers, grouped by area. Throughout Mnemo, shortcuts are written with the platform's own keys: Ctrl on Windows and Linux is Cmd on macOS.

## Rebinding

Each action offers **Edit shortcut**: press the keys you want and the new chord is captured. **Restore default** puts a single action back; a confirm-guarded reset restores everything at once.

If two enabled actions in the same area end up sharing a chord, the manager flags the conflict rather than guessing which one you meant.

## Editor shortcuts stay fixed

The editor's formatting chords, such as `Ctrl+B` for bold, cannot be rebound yet, and their rows sit disabled in the manager. **Restore default** still works on a locked row.

## Where the defaults are documented

Each area's defaults live with its docs:

- Editor formatting chords in [Formatting text](../modules/notes/formatting-text.md).
- Study session keys in [Study modes](../modules/flashcards/study-modes.md).
- Notes workspace keys in [Organizing your notes](../modules/notes/organizing-notes.md).
