Mnemo is available in English, Deutsch, Español, Norsk, and 日本語.

You choose a language during setup and can change it at any time under **Settings → General**. Changes apply immediately.

## Missing translations

New features may appear in English until their translations are added.

## Help translate

Translations are stored as JSON files in the [Mnemo repository](https://github.com/onemnemo/mnemo).

Corrections and new translations are welcome through GitHub.
