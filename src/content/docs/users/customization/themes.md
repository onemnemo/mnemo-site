Mnemo's appearance settings live in the app preferences. Pick whatever keeps you comfortable for long sessions; the best theme is the one you stop noticing.

## Light, dark, and system

The app can follow your operating system's appearance or pin itself to light or dark. If you study at night, following the system means the app dims when everything else does.

## A note on focus

Themes in Mnemo deliberately avoid loud accents in study views. Review sessions and the editor keep color for meaning (due states, links, selection), not decoration, so your attention stays on the material.
