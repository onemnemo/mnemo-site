The command palette

Press Ctrl K anywhere, or click Search at the top of the app. It finds note titles, deck names, pages and actions like toggling the theme or the sidebar. With nothing typed, it shows what you opened recently.

  • Start with > to see only actions.
  • Start with # to search deck tags.
  • Use the arrow keys and Enter to open a result, and Esc to close.

The palette doesn't search inside notes or cards. For those, read on.

Inside your notes

Ctrl P Find a note by title in the sidebar
Ctrl F Find and replace inside the note you're reading, with case and whole word options
Index The floating list of a note's headings. Click one to jump there.

To search the text of your cards, use Browse cards in Flashcards.

The side peek

The side peek keeps a note or card open beside whatever you're working on, so you can read one thing while writing another.

  • Hold Alt and click a note in the sidebar.
  • Focus a note and press Alt Enter.
  • Choose Open in side peek from a note's menu, or from a card's menu in Browse cards.

Notes in the side peek are for reading. Open full takes the note to the main view so you can edit it. Links to other notes open right there in the peek.

The peek can float over your work or dock to the left or right. Drag its inner edge to resize it. Esc closes it, unless you've pinned or docked it.
