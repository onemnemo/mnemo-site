Mnemo has a built in importer for Anki decks and Markdown, from a single note to a whole folder. Notion exports are trickier, so we made a small separate converter for those.

From Anki

In Flashcards, press Import at the top of the library and drop in your .apkg file.

Your cards come over with their fronts, backs, tags, clozes and images. So does your progress: due dates, intervals and review history all carry across, so a deck you've studied arrives studied. A deck called Spanish::Verbs becomes a Spanish folder with a Verbs deck inside.

Mnemo uses the same FSRS scheduling as Anki and reads your memory data where Anki has it. Afterwards it tells you which cards came with that data. Cards without it settle in over their next few reviews.

The same importer also takes CSV files with a front and a back column. Going back to Anki works too: export a deck as .apkg and it arrives studied.

From Markdown

In Notes, press Import notes at the top of the sidebar and pick a single file or a whole folder. To import straight into a folder you already have, right click it and choose Import notes here.

Each note takes its title from the file name, so rename files first if the title matters. Headings, lists, to dos, quotes, code, tables and equations all come through.

From Notion

In Notion, export your pages as Markdown & CSV. Run the export through our small Notion converter, which keeps your formatting intact, then bring the result in with Import notes like any other folder.

You'll find the converter on our GitHub.

When something already exists

If an imported note, deck or map has the same name as one you have, choose what happens: Keep both adds a number to the new one, Skip leaves yours alone and Replace overwrites it. Replace always asks first.
