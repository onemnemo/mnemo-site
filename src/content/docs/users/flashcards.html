Decks

A deck holds cards that belong together, usually one subject or course. A few broad decks work better than lots of tiny ones. Tags handle the finer sorting and turn into filters in the deck view.

Writing cards

  • One fact per card. If an answer has three parts, make three cards.
  • Ask a real question, something you can try to answer before you flip.
  • Use your own words. Copied sentences test recognition, your own phrasing tests recall.

Card types

Basic A front and a back.
Basic and reverse The same two sides, asked both ways.
Vocabulary A word, its meaning and an example. Makes up to three cards.
Cloze A sentence with blanks. Each blank becomes its own card.

For a cloze, select the words to hide and press Ctrl Shift C, or wrap them yourself: 

The {{c1::hippocampus}} consolidates memory during {{c2::sleep}}.

That makes two cards. Add a hint after a second pair of colons, like {{c1::sleep::stage}}, and it shows in place of the blank. Anything in the Extra field appears on the back of every card.

Ctrl Enter saves the card and keeps the editor open with the same deck, type and tags, ready for the next one.

Formulas and images

Write LaTeX between dollar signs, like $E_k$, or between double dollar signs for an equation on its own line. Each field holds up to three images: paste or drop them in. PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP all work, up to 20 MB each. Click an image during a review to zoom in.

Your own card types

Card types on a deck's menu opens the card type manager. A card type is a set of fields plus a front and a back template that use them, like {{Front}}. Card types are shared across your whole collection, and changing a template updates every card that uses it.

Keeping the library tidy

New makes a deck or a folder, and folders can nest. Drag a deck onto a folder to move it there. Click the icon beside a deck's name to give it an emoji.

Open a deck to see its cards in a table, with search, filters for due, new, learning, suspended and flagged cards, and bulk actions for moving, tagging, suspending and deleting.

  • Suspend takes a card out of every review without deleting it.
  • Flag marks a card for whatever you like. Flagged cards get their own filter, and you can flag during a review.

Search every card

Browse cards at the top of the library lists every card in one table, and is the only place that searches across decks. Every word you type has to match, and words match as prefixes, so cell mem finds a card about the cell membrane. Right click a row to edit it, move it or open it in the side peek.
