Mnemo works well out of the box, and most of it can be tuned in Settings.

Look and feel

Appearance Light, dark or match your system. You can also turn down motion.
General The language: English, Deutsch, Español, Norsk or 日本語. Changes apply right away.
Editor The width of the writing area, and autosave.
Keyboard Rebind most shortcuts. Mnemo warns you if two clash.
Notifications Turn off the popups. Messages still wait for you under the bell.
Profile Your name, picture and colour. It never leaves your device.

Updates

Mnemo checks for updates on its own and lets you update now or later. You can check by hand and read the release notes under Settings, then Updates.

Help translate

New features sometimes show up in English first. Spot a clumsy translation, or want to add your language? Translations live in the Mnemo repository, and help is always welcome.
