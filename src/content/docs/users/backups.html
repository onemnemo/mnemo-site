Make a backup

Go to Settings, then Storage & data, and press Back up. You get a single .mnemo-backup file with your notes, decks, maps, images, trash and settings. Sign ins and keys stay out of it.

To restore, press Choose backup. Mnemo shows what's inside before it does anything, then restarts to finish. Your old data is kept aside for a while in case you change your mind.

Where your data lives

Everything sits in one folder on your computer. Data folder, then Open takes you straight there.

Windows %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mnemo
macOS and Linux A Mnemo folder in your usual app data folder

You can also back up by copying the whole folder. Close Mnemo first. To use a different folder, set MNEMO_DATA_DIR.

Deleted something?

Right after you delete, an Undo button pops up. Missed it? Everything you delete stays in Settings, then Trash for 30 days. Press Restore to put it back where it was. Decks come back with their cards and review history intact.

Delete for good and Empty trash remove things for real, and both ask first.
